What XACO does

XACO opens any folder of JPEGs on your Mac and helps you organize them. As you browse, an on-device AI pass quietly analyzes each photo — recognizing scenes, reading text, finding faces.

Filter by smart category. Click any AI tag to narrow the grid. Dial the similarity slider to pull related photos together. Everything runs on your machine. Nothing leaves your Mac.

Tour

Smart sidebar

Tristate include / exclude filters for People, Text, Documents, Animals, Outdoors. Combine freely.

AI tags strip

Top vision labels for the focused photo. Click any tag to filter the grid. Sticks until you clear it.

Similarity stepper

Six thresholds from 95% to 60%. Click to find visually similar photos. Stricter on the left, looser on the right.

Search

⌘F searches filenames, AI tags, and any text the AI found inside photos (OCR).

Inspector

Right-side panel: file info, AI tags, OCR text, faces, plus rating, color threads, visibility, location.

Non-destructive

XACO never touches your originals. All edits and metadata live in plain JSON sidecars next to each photo.

Preview pane with the inspector — adjust tone, crop non-destructively, edit location.

Map view — every photo with GPS plotted as a thumbnail. Click any pin to peek the photo.

Install

  • Download XACO-Alpha-<version>.dmg from the Releases page.
  • Open the DMG, drag XACO into Applications.
  • Eject the DMG and launch XACO.

Signed and notarized by Apple, so first launch is smooth. If macOS prompts about a downloaded app, click Open.

Privacy & your data

XACO processes your photos entirely on your Mac. Nothing is collected, transmitted, or shared — about your photos, your usage, your Mac, or you. The app reads only the folders you explicitly open.

Your decisions are yours

Every deliberate thing you do to a photo — ratings, color threads, visibility, tone and crop adjustments, corrected dates, fixed locations — is written as a plain JSON sidecar file next to the photo. Fully inspectable. Readable in any text editor. Move the photo, the sidecar travels with it.

No proprietary catalog

AI-derived data (labels, OCR, face detections) lives in a local cache for speed — and can always be regenerated from your photos. Stop using XACO and your work goes with you. Nothing is locked in.
